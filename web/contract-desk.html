Every contract, from first draft to signed, in one desk.

Contract Desk keeps your agreements in one searchable repository, checks every redline against your legal playbook, routes approvals to the right people, and collects signatures. Open any contract and ask Claude about its renewal dates, notice deadlines and risks. Try it below with sample contracts.
