Auto-battle is on. Press any action to take control for 2 seconds.

Watch the timing bar over your rival's head. When the white needle enters the teal zone, Dodge to slip the hit and earn a counter (+50% damage). In the gold zone, Block for a Perfect Block that stuns them. You can cancel your own wind-up into a Dodge or Block.
