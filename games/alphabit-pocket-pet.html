Dew
Dewdrops drift across the screen. Tap one before it fades for 2 points. Quick catches in a row ring higher. Miss one, or let it fade, and AlphaBit is dismayed.
Feed
Fills food for 3 points. The sprout droops when AlphaBit is hungry, and a fed slime leaves a mess sooner or later.
Play
Raises fun for 3 points and costs a little rest. Petting the screen is worth 1.
Clean
Clears any mess for 4 points. AlphaBit gets annoyed while the floor is dirty.
Sleep
Lights off so rest can refill. No dew falls at night.
Grow
Points add up across days and AlphaBit changes as it ages: Toddler at 25, Teenager at 75, Adult at 175, Elder at 350.
Finish
At 500 points the journey is complete, about five days of play.
Goal
100 points fills the bar. After that you are done until tomorrow.
